# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to help. Argue about the work, not
the person doing it.

## What that means here

**Expected**

- Disagree with ideas, code and decisions freely — that is the job.
- Take feedback on your own work as being about the work.
- Assume competence and good faith. Most of what looks like a bad decision was a
  reasonable decision made with different information.
- Be patient with people who are new to this, to Postgres, or to English.

**Not acceptable**

- Personal attacks, insults, or making someone the subject rather than the code.
- Harassment of any kind, in issues, pull requests, discussions or private
  messages that start here.
- Demeaning comments about who someone is — including but not limited to their
  gender, sexuality, race, nationality, religion, disability, age or appearance.
- Sustained disruption: reopening settled decisions to make a point, or
  demanding unpaid work from maintainers.
- Publishing someone's private information.

## Scope

This applies in every space this project uses — issues, pull requests,
discussions, commit messages, and any chat or event held in the project's name.
It also applies when you are representing the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Email **hello@runbutter.app** with what happened, where, and links if you have
them. Reports are read by the maintainers and are not shared further without
your agreement, except where we are legally required to.

If your report is about a maintainer, say so — it will be handled by someone
else.

## What happens next

1. You get an acknowledgement, usually within a few days.
2. The maintainers look at the context and talk to the people involved.
3. Depending on what happened: a private word, a public correction, temporary
   removal from the project's spaces, or a permanent ban.

We would rather resolve something with a conversation than a ban. That is not a
promise that we won't ban.
